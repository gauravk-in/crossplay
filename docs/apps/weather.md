# Weather

Today's weather with an hourly strip, and the ten days after it. Apps folder,
`src/apps_local/weather/`.

## What it shows

- **Today**: the sky as a large icon, the temperature now, the condition, the
  day's high and low, the chance of rain, the strongest wind, sunrise and
  sunset.
- **The next 24 hours**, every three hours: icon and temperature.
- **Ten days**: the day, its sky, the chance of rain when it is 10% or more,
  and the low and high either side of a bar. The bars share one scale across
  the ten days, so a cold day sits visibly to the left of a warm one.
- **The footer** says when the report is from and whether it is being
  replaced: `UPDATED TODAY 08:15`, `UPDATING...`, `NO WI-FI. FROM WED 08:15`.

## When it fetches

Once per local day of the place, the first time the app is opened that day.
After that it shows the same report until refresh (top right) is tapped. The
day is the forecast's own: a forecast for Tokyo turns over at Tokyo's midnight,
not the reader's. The decision is `weather::freshness()`, and it also fetches
when the place has changed or when the device has never been told the date.

A fetch is one request to `api.open-meteo.com` (free, no key) for the current
conditions, 48 hours and 11 days. Wi-Fi comes up through the usual picker,
which joins a saved network by itself, and goes down again when the app is left.

## Settings

The gear (top left) opens them. Tapping a row changes it:

| Row | Values |
| --- | --- |
| Location | Search by city name; pick from up to eight matches (Open-Meteo's geocoding API) |
| Temperature | Celsius, Fahrenheit |
| Wind | km/h, mph, m/s, knots |
| Rain | mm, inches |

The forecast is stored metric and converted when drawn, so a unit change is a
repaint, never a fetch. Hours follow the reader's own 12/24-hour clock setting.

## On the card

| File | What |
| --- | --- |
| `/.crosspoint/weather.cfg` | `key=value` lines: the place (name, region, lat, lon) and the three units |
| `/.crosspoint/weather.txt` | The last report, `WX1` line format; see `WeatherCore.cpp` |

Place names are folded to ASCII on the way in (`Munchen`), because the Toybox
cuts carry nothing else and a missing glyph draws as nothing.

## Tests

`host-tests/weather/run.sh`: both file formats, the once-a-day rule (including
the place's midnight versus UTC's), units, WMO codes and the request URLs.

Icons are Lucide, generated by `tools_local/weather/gen_icons.sh`.
