BAMBULoading

Your printer

IP address On the printer: Settings, Network (WLAN on the A1). For example 192.168.1.42. A fixed address in your router keeps this from changing. Leave it empty to use only the Bambu account.
Serial number On the printer: Settings, Device. Also in Bambu Studio under Device, or on the sticker on the back.
LAN access code Eight characters, next to the IP address on the printer's network screen. It changes when LAN mode is turned off and on again.

The reader

Away from home

Bambu account Sign in to follow the printer through Bambu's cloud when the reader is not on its Wi-Fi. The reader keeps a sign-in token for about three months, never your password.
Printers on the account Tap one to use it, then save.

Last word

Served by the reader itself. The reader only reads the printer's status; it never sends it a command. Your Bambu password goes from this page to the reader and on to Bambu, and is not kept.
